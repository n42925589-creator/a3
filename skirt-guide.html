The full skirt & petticoat guide

Silhouettes, fabrics, petticoats and styling: everything you need to wear a full skirt with confidence.

Start here

Shape comes from what’s underneath

A skirt’s silhouette depends on three things: how it’s cut, the fabric it’s made from and what supports it. A circle skirt in soft jersey will drape close to the legs; the same cut in crisp cotton over a petticoat stands out in a bell.

Try the combinations in our silhouette studio.

Under layers

Petticoats, hoops and slips

Net petticoat

Layers of soft or stiff net gathered onto a waistband. The classic choice for 1950s and swing-dance looks.

Hoop skirt

Flexible boning rings stitched into a fabric underskirt. Gives the wide, smooth shape of a ball gown or historical costume.

Slip

A smooth, light underskirt that stops fabric clinging and makes sheer skirts opaque. Adds no volume.

Styling

Balancing volume

  • Keep the top fitted when the skirt is full.
  • Tuck shirts in and mark the waist with a belt.
  • Choose simple shoes so the skirt stays the focus.
  • Pair tulle with knitwear or denim for an everyday look.
  • Match the petticoat colour to the skirt, or choose a contrasting edge on purpose.

Do

  • Check that petticoats are shorter than the skirt.
  • Steam tulle gently to refresh it.
  • Sit by smoothing the skirt under you.

Don’t

  • Iron tulle or net directly; it can melt.
  • Pack petticoats tightly for long periods.
  • Stand close to open flames or heaters in full skirts.
