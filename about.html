About Crinoline Azure

An independent fashion guide celebrating full skirts, thoughtful silhouettes and the colour blue.

Our story

Why full skirts?

Few garments change how you move and feel like a full skirt. Yet the details that make one work, from the cut to the petticoat underneath, are rarely explained.

Crinoline Azure brings together practical guidance, a little fashion history and simple tools so you can find the shape, length and colours that suit you.

Practical

Advice you can use when shopping or dressing.

Inclusive

Styles for every height, shape and budget.

Brand-neutral

No sponsored content or paid placements.

Independent

We sell nothing and aren’t tied to any label.

Our tools, the silhouette studio, the length finder and the azure palettes, give general guidance only. Read more about how we research and write in our Editorial Policy.
